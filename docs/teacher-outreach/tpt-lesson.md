# ai and ay Spelling Words: UFLI Lesson 84, Grade 2 Week 7

A free one-page word list for elementary classrooms, built around
PatternSpell's chart at
https://patternspell.org/word-lists/ufli-lesson-84-ai-ay. No login,
no ads, no student accounts. Open the chart on a projector,
smartboard, tablet, or Chromebook.

Grade 2. Works for grades 1-3 as review or extension.

## What it is

PatternSpell shows spelling by pattern: one column per pattern,
all the words at once, so the class can read the whole chart
together. ai and ay both spell the long a sound. ai sits in the
middle of a word, ay sits at the end.

## The words

ai: rain, train, wait, snail, paint, chain

ay: play, day, say, away, stay, tray

## You will need

- 10 minutes
- A classroom display, or one device per student
- Nothing to print, the list is on this page

## The lesson

1. Show the chart. Open the page above and read the ai column
   together, then the ay column.
2. Listen for the pattern. Ask the class: what sound do ai and
   ay make? They both say long a. Ask: where does ay sit? At
   the end of the word. Where does ai sit? In the middle.
3. Sort it. Mix the words up, read them in a different order or
   write them on the board out of order, and have students sort
   them back into the ai column and the ay column.
4. Spell it. Say a word, students write it. Start with day and
   rain, then try away and snail.
5. Use it in a sentence. Pick a word and use it in a sentence
   together, then have students write their own sentence with
   one ai word and one ay word.

## Try these

Dictation: rain, play, train, day, snail, stay.

Then write one sentence with wait and one sentence with away.
