ai and ay Spelling Words: Teacher Instructions

UFLI Lesson 84, Grade 2 Week 7. A 10-minute lesson, no printing needed.

Start with the chart, not the printer.

patternspell.org/word-lists/ufli-lesson-84-ai-ay

Open that page on your classroom display and project the interactive chart. No printing needed, no login, no ads, and no student accounts. It works on projectors, smartboards, tablets, and Chromebooks.

What it is

PatternSpell shows spelling by pattern: one column per pattern, all the words at once, so the class can read the whole chart together. ai and ay both spell the long a sound. ai sits in the middle of a word, ay sits at the end.

The words

All 12 words for UFLI Lesson 84:

ai: rain, train, wait, snail, paint, chain

ay: play, day, say, away, stay, tray

You will need

  • 10 minutes
  • A classroom display, or one device per student
  • Nothing to print. The list is on the chart page, and the printables in this folder are optional.

The lesson (10 minutes)

  1. Show the chart. Open the page above and read the ai column together, then the ay column.
  2. Listen for the pattern. Ask the class: what sound do ai and ay make? They both say long a. Ask: where does ay sit? At the end of the word. Where does ai sit? In the middle.
  3. Sort it. Mix the words up, read them in a different order or write them on the board out of order, and have students sort them back into the ai column and the ay column.
  4. Spell it. Say a word, students write it. Start with day and rain, then try away and snail.
  5. Use it in a sentence. Pick a word and use it in a sentence together, then have students write their own sentence with one ai word and one ay word.

Try these

Dictation: rain, play, train, day, snail, stay.

Then write one sentence with wait and one sentence with away.
