ai and ay Spelling Words

UFLI Lesson 84, Grade 2 Week 7

All 12 words for UFLI Lesson 84: rain, train, wait, snail, paint, chain, play, day, say, away, stay, tray.

ai (long a)

  • rain
  • train
  • wait
  • snail
  • paint
  • chain

ay (long a)

  • play
  • day
  • say
  • away
  • stay
  • tray

Remember: ai and ay both spell the long a sound. ai sits in the middle of a word, ay sits at the end.

Worksheet: Write each word once

Word bank: rain, train, wait, snail, paint, chain, play, day, say, away, stay, tray

Answer key

ai: rain, train, wait, snail, paint, chain

ay: play, day, say, away, stay, tray

Try these: Dictation: rain, play, train, day, snail, stay. Then write one sentence with wait and one sentence with away.
