Description:

Spelling Made Fun with PatternSpell

A free, engaging online chart tool

Try it live: https://patternspell.org/word-lists/ufli-lesson-84-ai-ay

A printable ai and ay word list for Grade 2, built around
PatternSpell, a free chart tool. This folder has the lesson, a
black and white word list and worksheet, and a color pattern
chart.

ai and ay both spell the long a sound. ai sits in the middle of
a word, ay sits at the end. The chart shows one column per
pattern, all the words at once, so the class can read it
together on a projector or smartboard.

What is inside:

- 01 Teacher Instructions: the 10-minute lesson plan, website
  first, no printing required (2 pages)
- 02 Word List and Worksheet, Black and White: the word list
  plus a worksheet with a word bank, writing lines, and an
  answer key (2 pages)
- 03 Pattern Chart, Color: the pattern chart in color with the
  word list (1 page)
- All 12 words for UFLI Lesson 84, Grade 2 Week 7: rain,
  train, wait, snail, paint, chain, play, day, say, away,
  stay, tray
- Steps for showing the chart, listening for the pattern,
  sorting, spelling, and using the words in sentences
- A dictation list and sentence work to try

The tool is free, with no login, no ads, and no student
accounts. It works on projectors, smartboards, tablets, and
Chromebooks.
